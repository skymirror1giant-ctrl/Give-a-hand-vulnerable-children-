About Us

Give A Hand to Vulnerable Children is a community organization based in Kasese, Uganda. We are dedicated to improving the lives of children who have been affected by poverty, conflict, and lack of access to basic needs.

Our mission is to ensure every child has a chance to learn, grow, and thrive in a safe and loving environment.

Our Programs

📚 Education

Providing school fees, scholastic materials, and mentorship programs.

🍲 Food & Nutrition

Daily meals and nutrition support for malnourished children.

🏥 Health Care

Medical checkups, treatment, and health education.

🏠 Shelter

Safe housing and essential items for orphaned children.
